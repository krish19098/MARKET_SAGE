# Research Workflow

1. Translate the question into a precise research hypothesis or product decision.
2. Identify the minimum relevant data and its publication/availability timestamps.
3. Verify primary provider documentation, licensing and current coverage.
4. Separate observed facts, inferences, hypotheses and unknowns.
5. Define comparison groups, sample size, timeframe and possible biases.
6. For market research, use point-in-time data and avoid survivorship/look-ahead bias.
7. Record sources, assumptions, limitations and reproducible calculations.
8. Do not invent current market conditions, data coverage, performance or citations.
9. Convert findings into a testable implementation requirement.
10. Update project state and relevant reference documentation.
